### **Hi there!** 👋

I'm **Alfonso**, a **Software Engineer** based in Costa Rica with a background in **Electrical and Mechanical Engineering**.

I enjoy building systems at the intersection of **software and hardware**, with particular interests in:
- **C/C++ and Python Development**
- **Embedded and Low-Level Systems**
- **Linux and Computer Architecture**
- **VLSI and Digital Systems**
- **Hardware/Software Integration**
- **Low-Latency Systems**

This GitHub profile documents my ongoing development through personal, academic, and engineering projects, with an emphasis on **hands-on implementation, experimentation, and continuous improvement**.

---

### Featured Projects
- [VLSI Development Workspace:](https://github.com/alfonso-caoz/vlsi-development-workspace) Monorepo containing independent **VLSI projects**, organized around an **industry-like development workflow** using **Agile/Scrum** practices. Includes a reproducible **Docker-based EDA environment** for RTL development, simulation, verification, synthesis, and digital design - `Ongoing`.
- [MCU Traffic Light Controller - C:](https://github.com/alfonso-caoz/mcu-traffic-light-controller) **Bare-metal embedded** project implementing a vehicular and pedestrian traffic light controller on an **ATtiny4313**, developed in **C** using interrupts, timers, GPIO, and a finite state machine.
- [Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA) - C++:](https://github.com/alfonso-caoz/dsa-cpp) Ongoing **C++** development project focused on implementing and analyzing **data structures and algorithms**, with emphasis on STL usage, Big-O time/space complexity analysis, debugging, and automated testing with **Google Test (GTest)** and **CMake** - `Ongoing`.
